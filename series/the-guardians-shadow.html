Built on Skeat's Malay Magic (1900): the definition line that splits the water register between the inland Water Ghost and the Sea Ghost; the names-register of the sea-ghost's own family — Si Raya the father at the estuary, Madaruti the mother at the headwaters, Wa' Ranai the child in the middle reaches, one household stretched along the whole watercourse; the General Invocation's census of the deep — Imam An Jalil the sea's Imam, Bujang Ransang the sea's war-chief, elders below, in the cove, and at the cape,
