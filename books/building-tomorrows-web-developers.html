Building Tomorrow's Web Developers

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

A web address is a promise made to a stranger who has not arrived yet, and the trade's recurring difficulties start whenever that promise is filed as plumbing instead of a commitment. What follows describes the working reality of that arrangement: how the practitioners who build and hold the public-facing pages actually spend their weeks, what the territory covers, what the career genuinely pays, and what a machine that drafts an evening's worth of pages is changing for a trade whose product the whole outside world types into a box. Its 10 chapters move from The Address through The Long Arc. Written for people considering, entering, or managing web development work. Part of Building Tomorrow's People.
