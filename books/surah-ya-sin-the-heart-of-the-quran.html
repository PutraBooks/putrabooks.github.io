Surah Ya-Sin: The Heart of the Quran

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Surah Ya-Sin is the 36th surah of the Quran — 83 verses, revealed in Mecca, named after the two Arabic letters that open it. The tradition often calls it the heart of the Quran, a description that reflects what the surah actually does: in a single recitation it gathers the themes of the entire book — prophethood, rejection, warning, destruction, the signs in creation, the resurrection, the Garden and the Fire, and the return of everything to the One who created it. This book is a whole-surah reading: each chapter follows one block of verses in order, from the opening oath to the closing exaltation, examining what the verses say, how the scenes connect, and why this surah earned its name.
