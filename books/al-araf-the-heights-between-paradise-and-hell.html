Al-A'raf: The Heights Between Paradise and Hell

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Between the Garden and the Fire, the Quran describes a place that belongs fully to neither. In Surah Al-A'raf, in the passage that gives the surah its name, the text paints a scene from the Day of Resurrection: a partition between the two groups of humanity, and upon the Heights — al-A'raf — men who recognize all people by their marks, who greet the people of the Garden before entering it themselves, and who call out to the people of the Fire with words that are half warning, half grief: "Did I not tell you?"
