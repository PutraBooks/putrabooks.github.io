Average Hourly Earnings

S$1.90 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The payroll report has two headline numbers, and the second one — wages — is the line the Fed actually fears. Average Hourly Earnings trades the wage print honestly: the composition traps that distort the headline (the pandemic's distorted denominators, the hourly-versus-salaried mix, the workweek arithmetic), the surprise structure where a soft payroll print with hot wages sells the market anyway, the wage-price spiral watch and what the evidence actually showed, the reaction map for a wage surprise, and the real-time-versus-revised record. If you trade the jobs report but only watch payrolls, you're trading half the event — this is the other half, at the level of its arithmetic. Average Hourly Earnings is volume 12 of the Trading Impact News series: one market-moving release at a time, what it is, when it lands, and how traders position around it. DRM-free EPUB and PDF.
