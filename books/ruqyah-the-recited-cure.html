Ruqyah: The Recited Cure

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Ruqyah is the practice the Muslim world has always kept at the bedside: the recitation of the Quran over the sick, in the belief that the Book which heals the heart's diseases can also stand over the body's. It is old, widespread, and — like everything old and widespread — wrapped in a mixture of sound source-text and accumulating folklore. This book separates the two, following the shifa verses, the final three surahs, and the Prophet's own practice, with the one rule that drew its boundary: there is no harm in ruqyah so long as it contains no shirk.
