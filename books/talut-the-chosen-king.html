Talut: The Chosen King

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Talut: The Chosen King explores the story of Talut as the Quran presents it in Surah Al-Baqarah (2:246-251): the Israelites' request for a king, the divine appointment of Talut, the people's objection, the test of the river, the faith of the few who crossed, the battle with Jalut, the killing of the giant by Dawud, and the transfer of the kingdom. The book examines the criteria of divine leadership, the gap between intention and commitment, the theology of the small group, and the nature of victory by the permission of Allah.
