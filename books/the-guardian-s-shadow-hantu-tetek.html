The Guardian's Shadow: Hantu Tetek

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

A woman's form drifts into the room, her presence heavy and maternal — and her embrace kills. The hantu tetek, the breast ghost, is one of the stranger figures of Malay folklore: a female spirit whose breasts both lure and smother, said to take infants and grown men alike into a fatal embrace. This book traces her connections to the langsuir and pontianak, her role in village motherhood, and the warnings she carried for the vulnerable.
