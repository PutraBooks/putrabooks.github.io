Madyan: The Land of Shu'ayb

S$1.90 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran tells the story of a land of merchants — a people whose trade made them prosperous and whose prosperity made them corrupt. The land is Madyan, and the prophet sent to it was Shu'ayb. "And to Madyan [We sent] their brother Shu'ayb. He said: O my people, worship Allah. You have no deity other than Him" (7:85). Unlike 'Aad, whose sin was arrogance, or the people of Nuh, whose sin was rejection, Madyan sinned in its commerce: the short weight, the withheld measure, a market that priced people as profit. The book follows the whole account — the name and its place, the trade that shaped the people, Shu'ayb's call to honest measure, the rejection, the earthquake and the thicket, Madyan set beside the other destroyed nations, the lessons drawn from it, and the well of Madyan where Musa later arrived.
