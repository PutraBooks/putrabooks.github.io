The Day and Night: The Rhythm of Creation

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran mentions the day and night more than any other pair of phenomena. The alternation of light and darkness — *ikhtilaf al-layl wa al-nahar* — is mentioned in dozens of verses, always as a sign. The day is for work; the night is for rest. The sun is for the day; the moon is for the night. The light is for seeing; the darkness is for sleeping. The rhythm — on, off, light, dark, work, rest — is the most fundamental cycle in the natural world, and the Quran says: this rhythm is a sign.
