Jahannam: The Fire

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran describes Jahannam — the Fire — as the destination of those who rejected the truth, rebelled against the command, and lived in opposition to the will of Allah. The word *Jahannam* (جهنم) appears throughout the Quran as the name of the punishment that awaits the wrongdoer, the destination that is the consequence of the rejection, and the reality that is the contrast to the Garden. The Quran says: "And fear the Fire, which is prepared for the disbelievers" (3:131). The Fire is prepared — not hastily assembled but deliberately prepared, not accidentally encountered but intentionally designed — the destination that has been made ready for those who chose to reject.
