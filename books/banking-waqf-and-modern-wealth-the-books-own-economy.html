Banking, Waqf, and Modern Wealth: The Book's Own Economy

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran never wrote a banking manual — it wrote something larger: the frame every bank, contract, endowment, and ledger must stand inside. This book reads that frame in twelve chapters at the Book's own court: property as a held trust, never an absolute (57:7's successor-clause; 28:77's balance-verse); the interest-economy abolished in the Book's harshest commercial register — war from Allah and His Messenger on the persistent riba-eater (2:275-279) — while trade is chartered in the same breath; the profit-economy as a risk-economy (73:20's traveler, the fiqh's al-ghunm bil-ghurm, labeled); the debt-document of 2:282-283 — the longest verse in the Book — legislating the scribe, the witness, and the collateral fourteen centuries before the modern disclosure-regime; the amanah-deposit-economy (4:58, 8:27) and the orphan-estate's own fire-warning (4:2-10); the waqf as the charity that outlives the giver (the tree-file of 14:24-25, the enduring-good register of 18:46, the sadaqah jariyah, labeled); the kanz-line between saving and hoarding (9:34-35, 25:63-67, 3:180's necklace); the zakat as a claimed right collected on a schedule (9:103, 70:24-25, the eight-category budget of 9:60); the circulation-rule — wealth that must not loop only among the rich (59:7), read against the parable of the two gardens (18:32-44); the modern banking file itself — murabaha, ijarah, musharakah, sukuk, takaful (all labeled) tested at the Book's own four criteria: no return without risk, honest documentation, genuine consent, and outward circulation; and the wealth's yield — the loan-to-Allah economy and the seven-hundred-fold grain of 2:261, the multiplication the interest-economy counterfeits and the trust-economy actually receives. The Quran is the standing court; the tradition is labeled throughout; no fatwa is issued — the Book's own tests are read, honestly, against the economy we all now live inside.
