Friendship and Companionship

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Friendship and Companionship presents the complete Quranic guidance on choosing and keeping friends. Drawing primarily from the Quran, this book compiles the scattered guidance into a single coherent account — from the meaning of companionship (wali, khalil, rafiq, sadiq), through the believers as brothers, the choice of friends, the false friend who leads astray, friendship with the righteous, friendship across faiths, the rights of companionship, the loss of friends on the Day of Judgment, and Allah as the ultimate Companion whose remembrance brings rest to the heart.
