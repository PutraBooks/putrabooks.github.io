The Guardian's Shadow: Hantu Pisang

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

At dusk, where the banana trees stand in the kampung, the fronds rustle though the wind is still — and a woman may be standing beneath them. The hantu pisang, the banana-tree ghost, is a pontianak of the garden: a spirit said to haunt the trees and claim any who can tie a knot in her hair. This book traces the banana-tree legend, its connection to the greater vampire-ghosts of Malay tradition, and the quiet dangers of the garden at dusk.
