The Guardian's Shadow: Hantu Kum-Kum

There is a figure at the edge of the kampung's stories who is carrying a baby — and the baby is a tombstone. The modern record's one codified description of her is brief and exact: the Hantu Kum-Kum, it says, is "a singular entity," and she "looks like a woman carrying a tombstone as if it were a baby." An old woman, in the popular register, who takes the blood of the young to buy back her youth — the vampire of age itself.
