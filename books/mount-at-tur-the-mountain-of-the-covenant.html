Mount At-Tur: The Mountain of the Covenant

There are places in the Quran that host events, and there are places that are events. Mount At-Tur is both. The mountain of the Musa narrative — the mountain of the appointment, the call, the covenant, and the theophany — appears in the Quran in more than half a dozen passages, and in the most striking of them the mountain is not the setting of the event but the event itself: "And We lifted the Tur above you as if it were a canopy, and We said: Hold fast to what We have given you, and remember what is in it, that you may be God-fearing" (2:63).
