The Six Pillars of Faith in the Quran

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

If the five pillars of islam are the building's visible frame, the six pillars of iman are the ground the whole house stands on. The framework arrives from the tradition — the second answer of the Jibril-visit (labeled) — but the Book itself recites the creed at its own stations: 2:177, 2:285, and 4:136. This book reads the six in the Book's own text: the belief in Allah (112's catechism, 2:255's throne-verse, 3:18's witness-list); the angels (the worshipers of 66:6, the recorders of 82:10-12, the escorts of 16:32); the books (the named shelf of 5:44-48, 4:163, 87:19 — with the Quran as the library's guardian, 5:48's muhaymin); the messengers (21:25's universal commission, 4:163's roll-call, 33:40's seal, 4:165's mercy-architecture); the Last Day (22:7's certainty, 21:47's scales, 41:39's revival-evidence); and the decree (54:49, 57:22, 9:51's rest, 64:11's guided heart — with the scholars' balance against the two traps). Between the pillars: 4:136's surprise — believers commanded to believe (the maintained stance); the word iman itself (the amanah-root, 49:14-15's correction of the claim, 8:2-4's fruit-portrait); the growth-file (9:124's increase, the sickened-opposite of 2:10, the daily diet); the iman-islam relation (49:17's courtesy, the two words' one Owner); and the summit — ihsan: 'worship Allah as if you see Him' (labeled tradition) with the Book's muhsin-files (16:128, 2:195, 29:69). The three levels of the Jibril-scene held as the one house's three floors, ending at 15:99's closing frame: worship kept until the certainty. Companion volume to The Five Pillars (Lessons 16).
