Building the AI Ethics & Safety of Tomorrow

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Every machine that makes decisions acquires a safety culture - the question is whether it is built deliberately or discovered at the incident. This guide dissects the ethics and safety discipline for machine intelligence as engineering: the anatomy of a working program, a four-family harm taxonomy, the specification problem of saying what we actually mean, the evaluation of systems that cannot be fully tested, red-teaming and the adversarial layers, explanation and interpretability, privacy and data dignity, governance that can say no, deployment boundaries and incident response, and the operating discipline that assembles it all. A structural blueprint for building the AI ethics and safety of tomorrow.
