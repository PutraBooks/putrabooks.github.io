Sincerity: The Heart of the Deed

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran describes sincerity — *Ikhlas* — as the purity of the intention, the singularity of the devotion, and the exclusivity of the worship. The Quran says: "And they were not commanded except to worship Allah, sincere to Him in religion" (98:5). Sincerity is the heart — the heart of the deed, the heart of the worship, and the heart of the faith. Without sincerity, the deed is hollow, the worship is empty, and the faith is shell. The sincerity is the difference between the deed that is accepted and the deed that is rejected, the worship that is received and the worship that is returned, and the faith that is living and the faith that is dead.
