The New Sanctions Announcement

A sanctions announcement removes an economy from the dollar system — a severance enforced not by regulators but by every bank's compliance department, faster than any law. The New Sanctions Announcement trades the designation: the plumbing (the OFAC lists, the freeze mechanics, the fifty-percent rule that keeps catching offshore structures), the compliance cascade that makes the market impact arrive before the enforcement does, the general-license calendar and its carve-out trades, the commodity repricing channels through the case files from Rusal to the 2022 reserves freeze, and the exit discipline that keeps positions from becoming hostages. The event class where finance and geopolitics meet on a list — traded at the level of the machinery that enforces it. The New Sanctions Announcement is volume 16 of the Trading Impact News series: one market-moving release at a time, what it is, when it lands, and how traders position around it.
