The Guardian's Shadow: Pelesit

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Before the polong enters a house, the pelesit clears the way. A cricket-like spirit that hums and spins, the pelesit is the advance guard of Malay sorcery — the whisper in the night that precedes the attack. This book examines the little-known familiar of the bomoh tradition: its creation, its humming flight, its partnership with the polong, and the counter-rites that turned it back. A study of the smallest spirit with the largest reach in Malay magic.
