The Triumphant Valkyrie: The Personality That Chooses Glory and Treats Battle as Sacrament

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Ask this person to choose between comfort and glory, and watch the choice take no time at all. The triumphant valkyrie is a portrait of battle as sacrament — the personality that would rather be magnificent than easy, that treats the hardest assignment as the honorable one, and that comes alive exactly when the pressure arrives. The book reads the shine fairly: the courage is real, the excellence under fire is real, and the quieter types in any room owe this constitution more than they admit. It also reads the sacrament's costs — the peace that feels like dishonor, the ordinary Tuesday experienced as exile, the relationships that cannot survive a partner who keeps choosing the front line. The later chapters ground the warrior without dishonoring her: glory redefined to include the private, the beautiful that survives into peacetime, the valkyrie discovering that the last campaign is learning to be at home. An original personality study in the Archetype Series; it references no real persons, products, or properties.
