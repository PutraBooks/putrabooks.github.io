Plants of the Quran: Gardens of Meaning

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran mentions plants more than two hundred times. It names specific trees — the date palm, the olive, the fig, the pomegranate, the lote tree, the toothbrush tree, the gourd vine. It names categories — grain, vegetables, fruits, herbs, gardens. It describes the process of growth — the seed, the rain, the soil, the sprout, the stalk, the fruit. It describes the garden — both earthly and heavenly — as a place of shade, water, fruit, and rest. And it presents every plant, every tree, every grain as a sign — *ayat* — of the Creator who designed the seed, sent the rain, nourished the soil, and brought forth the fruit.
