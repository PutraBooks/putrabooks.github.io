Al-Jahim: The Fierce Fire

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran describes Al-Jahim — the Fierce Fire — as one of the names of the Fire, emphasizing the fierce, blazing, and relentlessly burning quality of the punishment. The word appears in the Quran in multiple contexts: "And for those who disbelieved in their Lord is the punishment of Hell, and wretched is the destination" (67:6, using Jahim in the broader sense). The name *Al-Jahim* carries the meaning of a fire that is aflame — a fire that is lit, blazing, and fierce in its intensity, a fire that does not subside and does not diminish.
