The house has one person who arrives exactly when the room is breaking. This book is about them — the gentle healer, whose mercy became a vocation before they were old enough to spell it. The portrait is tender and unsparing at once: the triage calm, the touch that steadies, the presence that mends, and beneath it the founding wound that taught the child that healing others was the safest room in the house.
