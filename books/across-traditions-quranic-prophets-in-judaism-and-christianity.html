Across Traditions: Quranic Prophets in Judaism and Christianity

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Across Traditions: Quranic Prophets in Judaism and Christianity is the second companion volume in the NurOne series. It compares the stories of prophets shared across the Abrahamic traditions: Adam and Nuh (flood and original sin), Ibrahim (covenant and sacrifice — Ismail or Ishaq?), Musa (exodus, the burning bush, prophetic character), Yusuf (the shared masterpiece narrative), Dawud and Sulayman (kingship and prophetic protection), Isa (divinity, crucifixion, and salvation), Yunus, Zakariya, Yahya, Ilyas, Alyasa, Lut, Ayyub, and more. The book explores the Quran's theology of prophetic protection ('isma), the universal message, the differences in genre and emphasis, and what the shared prophets reveal about the relationship between Islam, Judaism, and Christianity.
