The Retail Sales Report

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Two weeks after each month ends, the Census Bureau delivers the first broad read on what American consumers actually spent — and since consumption is roughly two-thirds of the U.S. economy, the print is the demand wing's flagship. The Retail Sales Report covers the read's construction (nominal, not inflation-adjusted — and why that distinction matters most in inflation eras), the control group the desks actually trade, the auto and gasoline distortions, the expectation market and the reaction map, the chain to GDP's consumption component, and the case files from the pandemic's violent swings to the resilient-consumer puzzle. The number that answers 'is the consumer holding up?' — how it's built, what moves it, and how to trade it without trading its noise. The Retail Sales Report is volume 9 of the Trading Impact News series: one market-moving release at a time, what it is, when it lands, and how traders position around it. DRM-free EPUB and PDF.
