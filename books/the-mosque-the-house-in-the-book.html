The Mosque: The House in the Book

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran legislates a whole House-architecture, and this book reads it in twelve chapters at the Book's own court: the First House established for mankind (3:96) and the Ibrahim-covenant to purify it (2:125, 22:26) — the founding-charter with its users-listed (the circumambulating, the secluding, the bowing, the prostrating); the purpose-file (24:36-37's houses of the remembered Name and the undiverted men); the unjust-block register (2:114) and the universal-Face correction (2:115); the call-economy (62:9's summons, 5:58's mockery-file, 8:24's life-call); the Friday-scene legislated whole (62:9-11 — the hastening, the trade-suspension, the dispersal-bounty, the caravan-drama); the congregation-economy (2:43's with-file, the row-file of the tradition, the salat al-khawf battle-prayer of 4:102-104); the dirar-contrast (9:107-110's false mosque against 9:108's Quba — the foundation as the building's own only true wall); the prevention-file (2:114's sentence, 9:17-18's custodian-credentials) and the pluralism-protection of 22:40 (monasteries, churches, synagogues, mosques defended as one landscape); the approach-manners (7:31's adornment, the mihrab-scenes of 3:37-39, the tradition's courtesy-file, labeled); the geography-file (17:1's Sacred-to-Furthest arc, 17:7's temple-history, 2:142-150's qibla-turning-scene); the builders-file (9:18's maintainers, the tradition's house-in-Paradise text, labeled, and the mosque-waqf economy); the enemy-and-neglect file (the Prophet's adorned-and-unattended lament, labeled); and the ownership-clause that closes it all: 72:18 — the mosques are Allah's. The Quran is the standing court; the tradition is labeled throughout; no fatwa is issued.
