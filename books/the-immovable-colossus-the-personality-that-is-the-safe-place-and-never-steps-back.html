The Immovable Colossus: The Personality That Is the Safe Place and Never Steps Back

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The household's storms all blew in one direction, and this person became the wall the frightened stood behind. The immovable colossus is the portrait of presence as structure: stability as the service the world extracts, the immovable voice in the crisis, the shoulder that has held more crying friends than it can count. The book studies the engineering with real respect: the calm is load-bearing, and everyone this type loves has leaned on it. It then studies the load honestly: the wall that never steps back because no one ever asked where it would like to stand, the feelings absorbed and never processed, the colossus quietly convinced that the only acceptable position is between everyone and everything. The later chapters do the renovation — the wall given doors, the steadiness kept while the self finally gets a room of its own, and the discovery that a safe place can also be a person who is sometimes allowed to shake. An original personality study in the Archetype Series; it references no real persons, products, or properties.
