The Guardian's Shadow: Putri Gunong Ledang

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

There is a mountain in Johor that the old charts called Ophir, and the Malay register calls Gunong Ledang, and both names keep the same tenant: a princess. She is the tradition's most named spirit — named in the Malay Annals, the court chronicle of the Malacca sultanate; named in Skeat's colonial-era record of Malay magic; named in the pawangs' own charms; named on the British admiralty charts as the reason a hill on the Selangor coast was worth marking at all; and named, still, in the modern retellings that carry her to the screen.
