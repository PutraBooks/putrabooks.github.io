Building the Tourism & Hospitality Infrastructure of Tomorrow

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

A hotel looks like a building with beds and a front desk. It is actually a machine for selling time: a room that is a perishable product expiring at midnight, a price that is a forecast repriced nightly, a service theater running twenty-four hours, and a destination economy underneath it all that lives or dies on the flow of visitors. This guide dissects that machinery layer by layer — the destination as infrastructure, the hotel machine, the guest journey from booking to departure, food and service layers, the travel machinery that brings the guest, revenue management and the price of a night, the hospitality workforce, safety and season risk, and the contactless, personalized re-foundation arriving at the front desk. A structural blueprint for building the tourism and hospitality infrastructure of tomorrow.
