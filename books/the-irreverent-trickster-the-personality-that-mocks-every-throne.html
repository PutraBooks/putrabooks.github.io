The Irreverent Trickster: The Personality That Mocks Every Throne

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Somewhere early, an authority figure's solemnity collapsed the moment a child laughed — and the child never recovered from the discovery. The irreverent trickster is a study of mockery as truth-telling: every crown examined on sight, most found funny, the emperor audited before lunch. The book takes the laugh seriously as an instrument of honesty — this type sees the nakedness that ceremony hides, and says so, and the room is better informed for it. It also takes the bill seriously: the sacred that never gets its day, the sincerity that would die of embarrassment, the thrones demolished indiscriminately, including the ones that were holding something up. The growth chapters refine the blade without dulling it — the trickster who learns what deserves the mockery and what deserves the bow, and finds, to everyone's surprise including their own, that the funniest person in the room can also be the truest. An original personality study in the Archetype Series; it references no real persons, products, or properties.
