The FOMC Interest Rate Decision

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Eight times a year, at 2:00 p.m. Eastern, a few hundred words reset the single most consequential price in the world economy — and repricing ripples through every currency, bond, equity, and commodity within a minute. The FOMC Interest Rate Decision is the reference volume for trading the Fed's biggest stage: the decision's construction (statement, dot plot, balance sheet), the expectation market that decides what even counts as a surprise, the reaction patterns — including the cut that rallies and the cut that sells off, and why — the statement-diffing discipline the desks actually use, and the case files from the 2018 pause pivot to the 2024 easing cycle. How to prepare, what to watch in the release's first seconds, and how the press conference thirty minutes later can rewrite what the statement meant. The FOMC Interest Rate Decision is volume 2 of the Trading Impact News series: one market-moving release at a time, what it is, when it lands, and how traders position around it.
