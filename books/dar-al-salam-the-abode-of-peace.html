Dar al-Salam: The Abode of Peace

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran describes Dar al-Salam — the Abode of Peace — as the destination whose defining characteristic is the complete, comprehensive, and unending peace. The phrase appears in the Quran in several key verses: "And Allah invites to the Abode of Peace and guides whom He wills to a straight path" (10:25). "For them is the Abode of Peace with their Lord, and He will be their protecting ally because of what they used to do" (6:127). "Their reward with their Lord is the Abode of Peace — gardens of perpetual residence, which they will enter. Beneath them rivers flow" (98:8). The word *Salam* — peace — is the name of the home, the quality of the home, and the experience of the inhabitant.
