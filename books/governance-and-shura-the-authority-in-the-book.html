Governance and Shura: The Authority in the Book

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran legislates a whole governance-architecture, and this book reads it in twelve chapters at the Book's own court: the sovereignty-frame (3:26's kingdom-as-loan doxology, 67:1's ownership-hymn, the provision-without-account tail); the counsel-command (42:38's shura-clause among the believers' own marks, 3:159's leader-order — consult, resolve, trust — with the Hudaybiyyah-leadership register, labeled); the trust-and-judge pair (4:58's amanah-and-qist charter with the key-occasion, labeled, and 4:105's courtroom-file); the obedience-register (4:59's ulil-amr chain with the tradition's honest debates, labeled, and the reference-court's constitutional-appeal); the hukm-files (5:44-50's three escalating registers, the exegetical court's positions labeled, the shirah pluralism-clause and the whims-warning); the justice-witnessing (4:135's against-yourselves clause, 5:8's enemy-file, 49:9's peace-court, the desire-warning's distortion-file); the pledge-economy (48:10's Hand-above-hands, the Ridwan tree-scene labeled, 60:12's women's-pledge creed, the Umar accountability-register labeled); the tyranny-warnings (Firawn's four-mark anatomy 28:4, Qarun's hoard-economy 28:76-82, the Thamud-watcher 91:11-15, the queen's counsel-table 27:32 and the envoy-file); the ruler's own courtroom (38:21-26's litigants-audit and the absent-party clause, 21:78-79's two-judgments refinement-theology, the Talut king-drama 2:246-249's merit-file and river-test); the public-administration (12:55's knowing-guardian, 4:83's information-file, 2:213's founding-polity); the correction-registers (the Prophet's own counsel-cases at Badr, Uhud, and Hudaybiyyah, labeled; 9:122's civic-education delegation; the Friday-rehearsal); and the kingdom-returned (2:258's Nimrod-debate, 3:189-190's ownership-clause, and 33:72's trust-carried — the believer's own citizenship at the Court's standing-oath). The Quran is the standing court; the tradition and history are labeled throughout; no fatwa is issued.
