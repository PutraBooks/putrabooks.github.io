The Seas: Waters of Wonders in the Quran

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran mentions the sea — *al-bahr* — more than forty times. It is one of the most frequently referenced physical phenomena in the entire text. The sea is mentioned as a source of food, a path for ships, a barrier between waters, a sign of divine mercy, a source of pearls and coral, a force that obeys divine command, and a body that will be set ablaze on the Day of Judgment. No other natural phenomenon in the Quran carries such a range of meanings.
