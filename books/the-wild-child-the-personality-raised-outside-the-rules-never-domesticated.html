The Wild Child: The Personality Raised Outside the Rules, Never Domesticated

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

This person was raised outside the fences — no schedule, no curriculum, no indoctrination into the indoor world — and the indoor people keep mistaking the result for rebellion. The wild child is a study of self-reliance as a native language: the instincts left unclipped, the comfort with the untamed that reads as chaos to anyone who has only lived in rooms. The book reads the constitution generously: this type survives things that finish domesticated people, sees exits where others see walls, and pays for freedom with a competence the rule-followers never had to build. It also reads the bill: the paperwork of the indoor world, the relationships that want fences, the loneliness of being fluent in a language the city does not speak. The later chapters build the bridge without building a cage: the wildness kept, the doorways learned, and the discovery that a free animal can also choose to stay. An original personality study in the Archetype Series; it references no real persons, products, or properties.
