The Nonfarm Payrolls Report

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Twelve times a year, on a scheduled Friday at 8:30 a.m. Eastern, the market's most explosive minute arrives. Traders know the number moves everything — almost nobody knows why it moves the way it does. The Nonfarm Payrolls Report is the reference volume for the jobs print: the two-survey structure and why the headline is built to disagree with the unemployment rate, the whisper-number expectation market, the reaction patterns — including the good-news-bad-news regimes where the same print rallies or wrecks depending on where the Fed cycle stands — the revisions and their documented skew, and the case files from the 2012 leak era to the post-pandemic payroll mysteries. The event's mechanics in full, so the fastest minute in markets stops being a coin flip and starts being a plan. The Nonfarm Payrolls Report is volume 4 of the Trading Impact News series: one market-moving release at a time, what it is, when it lands, and how traders position around it. DRM-free EPUB and PDF.
