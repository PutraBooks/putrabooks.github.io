The PCE Price Index

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Of all the events in this series, this one has the strangest profile: the price index that formally governs Federal Reserve policy — the gauge against which the 2% target is defined, the number projected in every SEP — is also, on release mornings, among the quietest numbers on the calendar. The PCE Price Index explains the paradox and trades it: what PCE measures that CPI can't (coverage, weights, the chain), why the Fed picked it, the CPI-PCE wedge and how the desks forecast this print from the louder one, the quiet-trade setups a calm calendar offers, and the SEP projections that put the gauge in policy context. For traders who want to understand what the Fed actually watches — and position ahead of those who only watch the headline number — this is the quiet volume that pays. The PCE Price Index is volume 6 of the Trading Impact News series: one market-moving release at a time, what it is, when it lands, and how traders position around it. DRM-free EPUB and PDF.
