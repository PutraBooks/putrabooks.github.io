An engineer is somebody who is trusted with a number that other people will stand on. This book is about the work behind that trust: what an engineer actually holds, what the trade has decided to call good practice, and how the work changes when the tools do. The chapters that follow take the trade apart in the same order as the rest of this collection. Written for people considering, entering, or advising a career in engineering.
