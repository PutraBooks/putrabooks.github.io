The Proving Defender

S$1.90 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Once, early, this person was dismissed — for size, age, or origin — and something in them signed a contract on the spot. The proving defender is the anatomy of overcompensation: smallness answered with force, the hammer that grew in proportion to the wound, the competence built precisely where the mockery landed. The book reads the defense generously at first, because it works — this type outperforms the doubters, protects the small, and refuses to be stepped on twice. Then it reads the terms of the contract: a life spent proving a case that was closed years ago, opponents recruited to keep the fight alive, the rest that feels like regression, the exhaustion of being permanently on trial before an audience that left. The growth chapters renegotiate the deal — the hammer kept but holstered, the size finally measured from the inside. An original personality study in the Archetype Series; it references no real persons, products, or properties.
