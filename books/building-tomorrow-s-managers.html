Building Tomorrow's Managers

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Management is the work every organization runs on and the craft the folklore has cheapened: the manager, mostly promoted for excellence at the previous work and left to operate by folklore - the bureaucrat's form-processing, the boss's instruction-issuing, the calendar consumed by the mechanics the machines are absorbing. This guide treats management as an operating craft rather than a title: the plan held as the operation's current theory - the goals decomposed into results the members can own, the resource map priced at the capacity's truth, the revision run at the rhythm the reality argues; the work designed as the operation's engineering - the process mapped from the reality, the handoffs built so the work survives the person, the standards owned by the hands that run them; the performance held honest - the expectation contracted with the member rather than assumed, the feedback loop run at the tempo the behavior can still change, the underperformance's decent path named early; the cadence engineered - the stations designed at the frequency the work's change-rate sets, the meetings priced at the room's honest multiplier, the translation held honest in both directions; the staffing built as capacity architecture - the roles designed before the vacancy forces, the arrivals assembled by the design's shape, the composition balanced as the portfolio the plan's future prices; the allocation run as the constraint's deliberate spending - the ranking published where the team can quote it, the tradeoffs priced openly, the escalations carried with the recommendation; the machine-era ledger held honestly - the mechanics delegated with the verification the checking funds, the signals read with the construction's audit and the walk's context; the middle's weight carried by craft - the translation's integrity, the account's honesty, the integrity the layers' pressures test; and the whole practice assembled on the path from managing work to managing managers. A structural blueprint for the management the machine era is short of.
