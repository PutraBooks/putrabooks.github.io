Gratitude: The Response to the Giver

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran describes gratitude — *Shukr* — as the response of the servant to the generosity of the Giver, the recognition of the blessing from the Bestower, and the acknowledgment of the gift from the Source. The Quran says: "And whoever is grateful is grateful for the benefit of himself" (31:12). Gratitude is the recognition — the recognition that sees the gift, the recognition that acknowledges the giver, and the recognition that responds with the thankfulness. The gratitude is not merely the feeling but the action — the action of the heart that appreciates, the action of the tongue that expresses, and the action of the limbs that use the blessing in the way of the Giver.
