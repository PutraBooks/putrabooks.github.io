A school is the machine that builds the operators of every other machine, including itself. This structural guide dissects the education infrastructure layer by layer: the learning machine's anatomy (the plant, the pipeline, the knowledge, the credential, and the control plane, held as one system); the compounding case for human capital as the slowest and strongest layer a country builds; the school plant (buildings, classrooms, time, and the engineering of attention); the teacher pipeline (the profession as the system's only irreplaceable moving part, from training to retention); the knowledge layer (curriculum, standards, and the system's software); the digital layer (the second classroom, bolted on and never substituted for the human core); the credential machine (universities, degrees, and the mint that prints trust); the distribution layer (finance, access, and the machine's equity engineering); the control plane (governance, ministries, and the system's truth layer); and the re-foundation - lifelong learning, the demographic clock, and the machine-era classroom. The twentieth volume of Building Infrastructure of Tomorrow.
