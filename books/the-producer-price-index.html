The Producer Price Index

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

A few days after mid-month, at 8:30 a.m. Eastern, the economy's wholesale-side price read lands — the cost at the point where producers sell rather than where consumers buy. The Producer Price Index trades the pipeline: final demand's construction (services, goods, and the trade-margin architecture most traders skip), the PPI-to-CPI transmission record — what leads, what lags, what dies on the way — the margin-squeeze channels that make the print an earnings signal as much as an inflation one, the reaction map, and the case files from the 2022 pipeline surge to its measured decompression. The inflation print that arrives first — read at the level of the machinery between it and the prices you pay. The Producer Price Index is volume 11 of the Trading Impact News series: one market-moving release at a time, what it is, when it lands, and how traders position around it. DRM-free EPUB and PDF.
