This page has moved to The Showcasing Prodigy: The trick came early, and the applause with it — and the applause was the only meal that ever satisfied. The showcasing prodigy is the portrait of genius run as a show: the feat deepening, the audience widening, the approval metabolized directly into identity until there is no self without a sightline. The book takes the talent seriously, because it is real and rare, and the rooms light up for a reason. Then it takes the economics seriously: the audience that must keep arriving, the effort disguised as effortlessness because effortlessness is the brand, the quiet dread of being seen practicing.
