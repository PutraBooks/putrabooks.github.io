Tabuk: The Last March

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Tabuk — the expedition of hardship — was the Prophet's last march: thirty thousand believers trekking to the Byzantine frontier in the summer's heat, at the harvest's week, while the hypocrites' quarter calculated in Madinah's shade. The battle never came; the campaign's real battlefield was the community's own heart, and the Quran's answer is At-Tawbah's central passage (9:38-127) — the ummah's complete audit. This book reads the whole passage and the event (tradition labeled throughout): the muster's theology ('go forth, light and heavy' 9:41) and the cling-diagnosis of 9:38; the excuse-files — the liars' leaves, the fear's own anatomy (9:56-57), the honest incapables exempted and honored; the slandered volunteers defended (9:79: the two date-baskets, the effort's percentage over the amount's figure); the forerunners' eternal rank (9:100: the first Muhajirun and Ansar, and those who follow them in good); the bargain struck (9:111: 'Allah has purchased from the believers their lives and their wealth — that theirs is Paradise... so rejoice in your bargain'); the three left behind — Ka'b ibn Malik's fifty-night boycott, the straitness despite the earth's breadth, the Ghassanid's burned letter, and the turn received (9:118); the harm-mosque classified, refused, and burned (9:107-110: 'never stand in it'); the mockers' file and the re-titling's cure (9:61: 'an ear of good for you'); the funeral at the grave's edge — ibn Ubayy's case, the Prophet's mercy exercised and the seventy-times' ceiling revealed (9:80); the weeping seven who found no mount, their tears filed in the eternal Book (9:92); and the legacy — the frontier's treaties, the delegations' transition, and the stayers' commission to learn and warn (9:122). The last march's bequest: the honest campaign, the wept exemption, the rejoiced bargain — portable to every era's frontier.
