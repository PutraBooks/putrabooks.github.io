Abu Lahab: The Uncle Who Rejected

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Abu Lahab is the only person condemned by name in the Quran. His real name was Abdul Uzza, but he was known as Abu Lahab — "Father of Flame" — for his fiery temperament and his reddish complexion. He was the paternal uncle of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him), the son of Abdul Muttalib, the brother of Abdullah, the uncle who should have been the protector but who became the enemy.
