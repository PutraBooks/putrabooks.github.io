Egypt: The Land of Pharaoh

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran tells the story of a land — a land of rivers, of palaces, of kings, and of trials. The land is Egypt — *Misr* in Arabic — and it is the setting of two of the most profound narratives in the Quran: the story of Yusuf, who rose from a well to the throne of Egypt, and the story of Musa, who rose from a river to the confrontation with Pharaoh. Egypt is the land where the human was tested and the divine intervened — the land where a slave became a minister and a tyrant was drowned.
