Luqman: The Wise

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Luqman: The Wise explores the story of Luqman as the Quran presents it in Surah Luqman (31:12-19): the gift of wisdom, the five counsels to his son (tawhid, dutifulness to parents, Allah's knowledge, prayer and action and patience, humility), and the question of whether Luqman was a prophet. The book covers the relationship between wisdom and revelation, the integration of theology and ethics, and Luqman as a model for non-prophet believers and for parents.
