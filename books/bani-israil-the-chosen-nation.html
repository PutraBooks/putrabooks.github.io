The Quran tells the stories of nations — peoples who received prophets, rejected or accepted the message, and were destroyed or saved. The first six books in this series covered destroyed nations: Qawm Nuh (the flood), Aad (the wind), Thamud (the thunderbolt), Qawm Lut (the rain of stones), Madyan (the earthquake), and Fir'awn (the drowning). This book covers a different kind of nation: Bani Isra'il — the Children of Israel — a nation that was saved, chosen, and tested.
