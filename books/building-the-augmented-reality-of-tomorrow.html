Building the Augmented Reality of Tomorrow

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Augmented reality is the discipline of putting computed things into the world you actually stand in - and holding them there: live compositing of virtual content into a live view of the physical world, registered to specific places, fast enough that the body accepts it. This structural guide treats the medium as infrastructure: what augmented reality precisely is and what its contract excludes, the anatomy of the six-stage pipeline and the time budget the body enforces, the registration discipline of world frames, anchors, drift, and relocalization, scene understanding as evidence about the world and the privacy of what the camera sees, the display frontier's optics, brightness, and weight, authoring the anchored layer with lifetimes, scopes, and owners, the interaction grammar across hands, gaze, and voice, the body's vetoes of comfort, attention, and endurance, deployment with an envelope written, rehearsed, and expanded on evidence, and the operating program with its five lines. A structural blueprint for building - and governing - the augmented reality of tomorrow.
