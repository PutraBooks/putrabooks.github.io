Patience: The Pillar of the Soul

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran describes patience — *Sabr* — as the quality that holds the believer steady in the face of trials, commands, and temptations. The Quran says: "O you who have believed, seek help through patience and prayer. Indeed, Allah is with the patient" (2:153). Patience is the pillar — the pillar that holds the soul in the storm, the pillar that supports the faith in the difficulty, and the pillar that maintains the steadiness in the turbulence. Without patience, the soul collapses under the weight of the trial, the faith crumbles under the pressure of the hardship, and the steadiness breaks under the force of the temptation.
