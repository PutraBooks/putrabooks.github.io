Building the Neural Networks of Tomorrow

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Neural networks are functions found by search rather than written by design - lattices of simple arithmetic units whose connection strengths were tuned by exposure to examples until the lattice did something useful. This structural guide treats the network as the infrastructure the decade stands on: what precisely a neural network is, the neuron, the layer, and the single function they compose into, the learning rule that grows behavior by error correction, architectures as recorded assumptions about the world, the data discipline that makes the corpus the true specification, scale and its honest budget, training runs operated as engineering projects, evaluation that keeps the measured number honest, deployment with envelopes matched to the measurement, and the operating program with its five lines. A structural blueprint for building - and governing - the neural networks of tomorrow.
