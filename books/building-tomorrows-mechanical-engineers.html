Building Tomorrow's Mechanical Engineers

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

A machine is understood only within the conditions somebody declared for it, and the declaration is the work. What follows concerns how mechanical engineering is actually practised day to day: the habits its people rely on, the way the specialisms split, what the career path pays out for, and the shifts that arrive as design and testing tools outrun the human judgement operating them. Its 10 chapters move from The Envelope through The Long Path. Written for people considering, entering, or advising a career in mechanical engineering. Part of Building Tomorrow's People.
