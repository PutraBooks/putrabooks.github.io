Dhul-Qarnayn: The Just King

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Dhul-Qarnayn: The Just King explores the story of Dhul-Qarnayn as the Quran presents it: three journeys (to the westernmost, easternmost, and between two mountains), the building of the barrier against Gog and Magog, the refusal of tribute, the acknowledgment of the Hereafter, and the recognition that all achievement is temporary before the divine promise. The book covers the Quran's model of just kingship, the relationship between material construction and spiritual meaning, and the limits of human power.
