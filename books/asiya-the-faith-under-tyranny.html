Asiya: The Faith Under Tyranny

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Asiya: The Faith Under Tyranny explores the story of Asiya, wife of Pharaoh, as the Quran presents her: the woman who saved Musa, believed in Allah in the palace of the tyrant, and prayed the prayer that defines her — 'My Lord, build for me near You a house in Paradise.' The book covers the context of Pharaoh's court, the prayer for Paradise, the contrast between Asiya and Pharaoh, the choice of faith over power, the pairing of Asiya and Maryam as examples for all believers, the parallel with the believing man of Pharaoh's household, Asiya's role as Musa's protector, and lessons on faith, tyranny, prayer, and patience.
