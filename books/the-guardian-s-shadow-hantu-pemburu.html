The Guardian's Shadow: Hantu Pemburu

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Deep in the Malay jungle, hunters sometimes hear what they dread most: baying dogs, hoofbeats, a horn — the hantu pemburu, the Wild Hunt of Malay tradition, riding through the trees. To see the spectral hunter is not a story; it is a warning. This book traces the hunting ghost from his possible origins in history and legend, the taboos of answering his call, and the eerie parallels to Europe's Wild Hunt.
