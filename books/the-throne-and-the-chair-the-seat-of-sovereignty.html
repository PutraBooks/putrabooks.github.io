The Throne and the Chair: The Seat of Sovereignty

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Throne and the Chair: The Seat of Sovereignty explores the Quranic teachings about the greatest of Allah's creation: the Throne (al-Arsh) as the symbol of divine sovereignty, the Chair (al-Kursi) as the scope of divine knowledge as described in Ayat al-Kursi (2:255), the distinction between the two, the angels who bear the Throne and their supplication for the believers, the Throne as the roof of the universe, the Throne on the Day of Judgment as the seat of justice, the believer's nearness to the Throne, Sulayman's throne as a human reflection of divine kingship, and the lessons these realities teach about the greatness, mercy, and sovereignty of Allah.
