The CPI Report

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

For roughly two years, the monthly CPI release was the single most consequential event in global finance — ahead of the Fed's meetings, ahead of the jobs report. Inflation eras return; this is the volume for them. The CPI Report covers the print's construction (headline, core, shelter's lag, the base-effect calendar), the expectation market and why a 0.1 miss can move thousands of points, the reaction regimes from the inflation-scare cycle to the disinflation rally, the reaction map that decodes why the same print buys stocks one month and sells them the next, and the case files from the 2021 'transitory' prints through the 2023 cooling. The defining market number of the decade — how it's built, what it means, and how it's traded when it matters most. The CPI Report is volume 5 of the Trading Impact News series: one market-moving release at a time, what it is, when it lands, and how traders position around it. DRM-free EPUB and PDF.
