The Guardian's Shadow: Mambang

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

At the edge of the forest, lights bob and weave — gold, green, red — and the old people in Kelantan knew better than to follow them. The mambang are spirits of colour and light, neither wholly good nor evil, capable of leading travellers astray or of granting favours to those with the proper approach. This book traces the light-spirits of Malay tradition: the kinds of mambang, the taboos around following them, and the ceremonies that spoke to them.
