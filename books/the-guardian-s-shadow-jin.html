The Guardian's Shadow: Jin

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Made of smokeless fire, unseen, older than the hills — the jin are not ghosts but a second creation: beings of free will who marry, quarrel, believe, and haunt. In the Malay world, Islam and folk tradition meet in the jin: the possessors, the contract-spirits, the dwellers of termite mounds and drains. This book examines the jin of the Quran and the kampung: their kinds and ranks, possession and exorcism, the rules of coexistence, and the line between faith and folklore.
