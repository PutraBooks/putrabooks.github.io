Purity and Cleanliness: The Book's Own Cleanliness

The Quran legislates purity in tiers, and this book reads the whole thaharah-architecture at the Book's own court: the partial wash of wudu with its four limbs (5:6) and its mercy-tail; the minor state — hadath asghar — with the tradition's nullifier-tables (labeled): the reliefs, the wind, the deep sleep, the famous touch-debates reported as the schools' own positions; the full bath of ghusl (4:43's junub-clause, the contact-and-emission boundary-cases reported honestly); the major state — hadath akbar — with its trigger-tables (labeled): the sexual contact, the mani-emission (keluar mani, awake or asleep, with the ihtilam-case's blameless register), menstruation's end, the postpartum file; the private-parts economy of 23:5-7 and 70:29-31 (the marriage-exception and the transgressor-clause), the gaze-file of 24:30-31, and the madhhab's own debated rulings on masturbation — reported honestly as the labeled positions they are, with 39:53's mercy-gate and 3:135's register standing open at the chapter's end; the menstruation-legislation of 2:222 (the adha-diagnosis, the conjugal scope-block, the return at the purification, the cultural-exile file corrected); the najis-file at 74:4-5's garment-command and 8:11's rain-charter with the fiqh's categories (labeled); tayammum at 4:43 and 5:6 (the waterless and harm cases, 9:108's Quba-register, the wound-and-khuff case-law, labeled); the water-file (8:11's Badr-rain, the sea-register, the well-cases, labeled); and the pure heart — the tazkiyah-destination of the whole architecture (56:79, 91:9-10, 87:14-15, 3:106-107's bright-face economy). The Quran is the standing court; the fiqh is labeled throughout; no fatwa is issued.
