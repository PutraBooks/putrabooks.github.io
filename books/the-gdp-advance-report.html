The GDP Advance Report

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Once a quarter, the market's biggest question — how fast is the economy growing? — gets its first answer, and the market mostly shrugs. Why? Because the GDP advance estimate is a decomposition, a revision factory, and a trade the desks have learned to time differently. The GDP Advance Report covers: the expenditure components and which one actually carries the print (net exports, the notorious wild card), the advance-second-third revision ladder and its documented errors, the reaction patterns — the goldilocks print, the boom that fears the Fed, the recession-confirm — and the case files from the pandemic-era swings to the inventory-distorted prints. What the number measures, what it can't, and the few honest trades it offers: the economy's scorecard, read at the level of its error bars. The GDP Advance Report is volume 7 of the Trading Impact News series: one market-moving release at a time, what it is, when it lands, and how traders position around it. DRM-free EPUB and PDF.
