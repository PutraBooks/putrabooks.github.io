Building Tomorrow's Entrepreneurs

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The folklore teaches the founding as a story - the spark, the leap, the exit - and the record keeps showing something else: a decade of decisions, each with its own evidence, failure modes, and instruments. This guide treats the entrepreneur's whole arc as an operating discipline: the founding decision made deliberately through the motive, position, and fit audits; the idea tested before it is built on the evidence ladder; the co-founder chosen by protocol and the founders' agreement argued while it is cheap; the first customer read as evidence, priced honestly, and served into a reputation; the capital journey raised from needs rather than fashion, with the founder's own ledger kept alongside the venture's; the build run as a hypothesis with a designed feedback loop; the scaling held at its gate until the economics, repeatability, and capacity are real; the founder's role rebuilt at every stage before the stage forces it; the hard times run on arithmetic rather than adrenaline; and the exit designed years before it is priced. A structural blueprint for the founders the decade will be built by.
