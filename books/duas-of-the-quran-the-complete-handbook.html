Duas of the Quran: The Complete Handbook

Duas of the Quran: The Complete Handbook is the companion volume to Du'a: Supplication in the Quran. Where Book 13 covered the framework of supplication, this book is the collection: every major dua of the Quran gathered and organized by human need. Musa's dua for provision when starting from zero. Ayyub's one-sentence call in total loss. Yunus's confession in the belly of the whale — the dua the Prophet said is never refused. Nuh's teaching that seeking forgiveness itself brings rain, wealth, and children. The protective surahs against envy, sorcery, and whispering. The duas for debt and hardship, for children and parents, for the good end and the house in Paradise. Each entry gives the words, the source story, when to use it, and why it works.
