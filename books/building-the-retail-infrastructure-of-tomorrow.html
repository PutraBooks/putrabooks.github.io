Building the Retail Infrastructure of Tomorrow

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

A store looks like a room full of things for sale. It is actually one of the most engineered machines in the economy: a demand-forecasting engine, a distribution network compressed onto shelves, a pricing system that reprices thousands of items every day, a checkout that clears hundreds of transactions an hour, and a supply chain that lands the right product on the right shelf before the customer knows she wants it. This guide dissects that machinery layer by layer — the store as a machine, distribution and replenishment, inventory's working memory, pricing and the markdown engine, the checkout rails, the omnichannel merge, the people systems, shrink and failure modes, and the autonomous-store re-foundation arriving at the shelf. A structural blueprint for building the retail infrastructure of tomorrow.
