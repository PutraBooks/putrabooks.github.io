Lazaa: The Blazing Flame

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran describes Lazaa — the Blazing Flame — as one of the names of the Fire, emphasizing the intensity, the ferocity, and the consuming nature of the punishment. The word appears in the Quran: "By no means! Indeed, it is a Flame of fire, which strips away the scalp" (70:15-16). The name *Lazaa* carries the meaning of a fire that blazes without restraint — a flame that consumes with intensity, a blaze that burns with ferocity, and a fire that leaves nothing untouched.
