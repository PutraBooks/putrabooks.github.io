Children: The Trust We Carry presents the complete Quranic guidance on raising and caring for children. Drawing primarily from the Quran, this book compiles the scattered guidance into a single coherent account — from the gift of children as a trust, through the mother's sacrifice of pregnancy and nursing, the father's obligations of provision and protection, the duty of kindness to parents, education and discipline, the community's responsibility toward orphans, and the painful test of losing a child.
