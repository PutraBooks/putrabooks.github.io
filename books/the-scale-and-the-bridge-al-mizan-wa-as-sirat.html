The Scale and the Bridge: Al-Mizan wa As-Sirat

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran describes two critical stages of the Day of Judgment: the Scale — *Al-Mizan* — on which the deeds are weighed, and the Bridge — *As-Sirat* — over which the souls must cross. The Quran says: "And We place the scales of justice for the Day of Resurrection, so no soul will be treated unjustly at all. And if there is even the weight of a mustard seed, We will bring it forth. And sufficient are We as accountant" (21:47). The Scale is the measure — the measure that is precise, the measure that is just, and the measure that is complete. The Bridge is the passage — the passage that is tested, the passage that is challenged, and the passage that determines the final destination.
