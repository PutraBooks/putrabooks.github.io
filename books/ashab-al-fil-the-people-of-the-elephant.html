Ashab al-Fil: The People of the Elephant

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran tells the story of a army that came to destroy the House of Allah and was destroyed by Allah before it could reach it. The story is told in five verses — five short, powerful verses that describe the event with precision and finality: "Have you not seen how your Lord dealt with the companions of the elephant? Did He not make their plan into misguidance? And He sent against them birds in flocks, striking them with stones of baked clay, and He made them like eaten straw" (105:1-5).
