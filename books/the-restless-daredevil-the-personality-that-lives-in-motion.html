The Restless Daredevil: The Personality That Lives in Motion

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Stillness is not this person's first language; motion is. The restless daredevil is recognizable mid-air — between jobs, cities, ideas — and misread as flighty by people who have never watched the landing. This book takes the type seriously on the type's own terms: speed as cognition, the present tense as a genuine gift, the appetite for edge as a search for something the ordinary week does not supply. It also counts what the motion spends — the relationships restarted rather than repaired, the commitments that die of scheduled boredom, the roots never tested because they were never planted. The arc of the second half is the arc of the daredevil who stays by choice: what remains when the engine is finally still, and why that remaining turns out to be the boldest move of all. An original personality study in the Archetype Series; it references no real persons, products, or properties.
