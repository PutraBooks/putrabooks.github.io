The Guardian's Shadow: Jembalang

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Before a village could dig a well, clear a field, or raise a house, offerings had to go into the ground first — for the jembalang, the earth spirits who own the soil. Disturb their land without permission and the ground itself takes offense: collapses, fever, misfortune. This book explores the Malay-Kampung tradition of the ground's guardians: the rituals of first-fruits and coloured rice, the construction taboos, and the enduring etiquette of building on borrowed earth.
