The Earth: Mountains, Oceans, and Rivers in the Quran

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Beneath your feet is a sign. The Quran does not say this once or twice — it says it dozens of times, in different ways, through different images. The earth is a *firash*, a carpet spread out. The mountains are *awtad*, pegs driven into the ground. The rivers are paths carved through the land. The oceans are bodies of water held back by an unseen barrier. The soil is the source of every plant, every tree, every grain of food. The earth is not a dead rock spinning in space; it is a living sign, a speaking testimony, a book as readable as the Quran itself.
