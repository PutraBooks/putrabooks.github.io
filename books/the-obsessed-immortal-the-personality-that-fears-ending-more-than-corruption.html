The Obsessed Immortal: The Personality That Fears Ending More Than Corruption

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Somewhere early, ending arrived too personally — and this person decided, quietly, never to be done. The obsessed immortal is the study of the indefinite deferral: every finish line moved, every ending renegotiated, the account kept open at any cost because closing it is the enemy. The book reads the fear at its root: mortality met young, and the projects, wars, and careers that became immortality machines. It reads the currency honestly, too: the endings that were actually beginnings wearing disguises, the completions declined like parcels, the colleagues and loved ones who keep attending rehearsals of things that never premiere. The later chapters do the unthinkable gently: one small thing, finished, on purpose, survived — and the discovery that an ending is not a death but a delivery, and that the self, completed, does not stop. An original personality study in the Archetype Series; it references no real persons, products, or properties.
