Al-Hawiyah: The Abyss

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran describes Al-Hawiyah — the Abyss — as one of the names of the Fire, emphasizing the depth, the devouring, and the falling quality of the punishment. The word appears in Surah al-Qari'ah: "And as for one whose scale is light, his home will be Hawiyah. And what will make you know what it is? A fire, intensely hot" (101:8-11). The name *Al-Hawiyah* carries the meaning of a fire that is an abyss — a deep, bottomless pit that swallows those whose good deeds are weightless, whose wrong deeds are heavy, and whose lives have been lived in the denial of the truth.
