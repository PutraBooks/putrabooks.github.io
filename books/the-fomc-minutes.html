The FOMC Minutes

S$1.90 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Three weeks after every FOMC meeting, the Fed publishes the minutes — the delayed record of what the committee actually discussed behind doors the statement closed — and rates desks around the world stop for a document about a decision made weeks ago. The FOMC Minutes explains why the afterword still moves: the minutes as a committee-sentiment telescope (the debate texture, the dissents, the 'various' versus 'several' vocabulary the desks parse), the patterns where the minutes telegraphed the next meeting's move, the second-look trade setups, and the case files where three-week-old words repriced the present. How to read the minutes like an FOMC insider, what to look for first, and how a document about the past becomes information about the future — the Fed's slow leak, decoded. The FOMC Minutes is volume 8 of the Trading Impact News series: one market-moving release at a time, what it is, when it lands, and how traders position around it. DRM-free EPUB and PDF.
