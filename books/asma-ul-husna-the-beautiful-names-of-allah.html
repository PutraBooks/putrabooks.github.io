Asma ul-Husna: The Beautiful Names of Allah

Asma ul-Husna: The Beautiful Names of Allah completes the command of 7:180 — 'to Allah belong the most beautiful names, so call upon Him by them.' The Book never lists its names; it teaches them in context. This book gathers what it declares: the Name above the names (Allah), the mercy pair that opens every surah (Ar-Rahman, Ar-Rahim), the oneness family (Al-Wahid, Al-Ahad, As-Samad, Al-Haqq), the sovereignty crown of 59:23 (Al-Malik, Al-Quddus, As-Salam, Al-Mu'min, Al-Muhaymin, Al-Aziz, Al-Jabbar, Al-Mutakabbir), the creation trilogy of 59:24 (Al-Khaliq, Al-Bari', Al-Musawwir, with Al-Badi' and Al-Fatir), the sustenance names (Ar-Razzaq, Al-Wasi', Al-Wahhab, Al-Ghaniyy, Al-Karim, Al-Akram), the knowledge family (Al-Alim, Al-Khabir, Ash-Shahid, As-Sami', Al-Basir, Al-Latif), the power names (Al-Qawiyy, Al-Matin, Al-Qadir, Al-Qahhar, Al-Fattah, Al-Azim, Al-Aliyy, Al-Kabir), the forgiveness family that keeps the door open (Al-Ghafur, At-Tawwab, Al-Halim, Ash-Shakur, Al-Wadud, Ar-Ra'uf), the nearness names (Al-Hayy, Al-Qayyum, An-Nur, Al-Barr, Al-Mawla), and the eternal frame (Al-Awwal, Al-Akhir, Az-Zahir, Al-Batin, Al-Majid, Al-Hamid) — closing with the calling: how the names become the believer's dua vocabulary, pairing every need to the name that answers it. Only Quran-declared names; the tradition's wider lists labeled as tradition.
