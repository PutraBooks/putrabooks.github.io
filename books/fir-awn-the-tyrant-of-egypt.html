Fir'awn: The Tyrant of Egypt

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

He commanded the killing of every Israelite son and told his court, 'I know no god for you but me.' Fir'awn is the Quran's most detailed portrait of tyranny: the nine signs, the magicians who believed, the sea that parted and closed again, and the faith that came too late to save him. It covers the most detailed destroyed nation in the Quran — Pharaoh's Egypt. The book traces Pharaoh's claim to divinity, the machinery of his system (the hierarchy, the magicians, the factionalism, the exploitation), Musa's mission and confrontation in the court, the nine signs and Pharaoh's repeated rejection, the contest with the magicians and their conversion, the hidden believer who spoke up, the night of the exodus, the parting of the sea, the drowning of Pharaoh and his army, the preserved body as a sign, Pharaoh as the paradigm of tyranny in the broader Quranic narrative, and reflections on power, the system, and the timely faith.
