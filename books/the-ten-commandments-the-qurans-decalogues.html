The Ten Commandments: The Quran's Decalogues

S$1.90 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran contains its own ten-commandments passages — two of them, framed as complete recitations of the Lord's core demands. The first: "Say: come, I will recite what your Lord has made sacred to you" (6:151-153) — ten commands in three verses, ending at "and this is My path, a straight one." The second: "Your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him" (17:23-39) — the Bani Israil-version, longer, ending at the idols'-return warning. And behind both stands the Torah's own tablets: "and We wrote for him on the tablets" (7:145) — the earlier decalogue the Quran remembers and confirms.
