The Judgment: Al-Hisab

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran describes the Judgment — *Al-Hisab* — as the Day when every soul is held accountable for what it did, when every deed is examined, and when every person faces the record of their life. The Quran says: "And fear a Day when you will be returned to Allah. Then every soul will be paid for what it earned, and they will not be wronged" (2:281). The Judgment is the accounting — the accounting that is precise, the accounting that is complete, and the accounting that is just. It is the moment when the life is reviewed, the deeds are weighed, and the consequence is determined.
