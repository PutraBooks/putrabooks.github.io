Jannat al-Firdaws: The Highest Paradise

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Jannat al-Firdaws — the Highest Paradise. The name itself carries a weight that no translation can fully capture. *Firdaws* is a word of Persian origin that entered Arabic through the Quran, and it signifies the summit of all gardens, the apex of all reward, the place where the descriptions of paradise converge and reach their fullest expression. When the Quran speaks of paradise, it uses many names — Jannat 'Adn, Jannat al-Na'im, Dar al-Salam — but Firdaws stands above them all. It is not merely one garden among many; it is the garden that contains the Throne, the garden from which the rivers of paradise flow, the garden that the Prophet Muhammad described as the highest level, the most excellent of all paradises.
