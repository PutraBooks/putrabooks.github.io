Weather as Signs: Rain, Thunder, and Divine Mercy

The Quran mentions weather more than any other natural phenomenon. Rain, clouds, thunder, lightning, wind, hail — all are described, analyzed, and presented as signs of the Creator. The weather is the Quran's most accessible sign because it is the most universal: every human being, in every land, in every era, experiences weather. The rain that falls on the desert Arab falls on the city dweller, the farmer, the nomad, the king. The weather is the sign that no one can escape.
