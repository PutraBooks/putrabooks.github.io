The Hollow Prophet: The Personality That Sells Hope It Does Not Have

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The sermon lands every time — and the preacher goes home cold. The hollow prophet is the hardest study in this stretch of the series: the person who discovered, young, that selling hope kept a desperate household functional, and who has been delivering the prophecy with total conviction ever since without checking whether the shop stocks any. The book reads the gift seriously: this type moves rooms, steadies panics, and functions as infrastructure for other people's faith. Then it reads the hollow behind the pulpit: the fuel that warms everyone except the prophet, the performance that has no green room, the despair managed by never once being offstage. The later chapters attempt the rarest exit: the prophet audited by an honest inventory, the sermon re-founded on something the speaker actually believes, and the strange, difficult discovery that hope, said by someone who finally has some, works better. An original personality study in the Archetype Series; it references no real persons, products, or properties.
