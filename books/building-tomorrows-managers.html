Building Tomorrow's Managers

Management is the work every organization runs on and the craft the folklore has cheapened: the manager, mostly promoted for excellence at the previous work and left to operate by folklore - the bureaucrat's form-processing, the boss's instruction-issuing, the calendar consumed by the mechanics the machines are absorbing. This guide treats management as an operating craft rather than a title: the plan held as the operation's current theory; the work designed so the results survive the person; the performance held honest at the feedback's operating tempo; the cadence engineered at the frequency the work's change-rate sets; the staffing built as capacity architecture; the allocation run as the constraint's deliberate spending; the machine-era ledger held honestly; the middle's weight carried by craft; and the whole practice assembled on the path from managing work to managing managers. A structural blueprint for the management the machine era is short of. Want to read before you buy?
