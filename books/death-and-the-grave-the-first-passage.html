Death and the Grave: The First Passage

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran describes death not as an end but as a passage — the transition from one form of existence to another, the movement from this world to the next, and the beginning of the journey toward the final destination. The Quran says: "Every soul will taste death, and you will only be paid your full reward on the Day of Resurrection" (3:185). Death is the certainty that every soul shares, the threshold that every person crosses, and the moment that every life reaches. It is the first passage of the Hereafter — the doorway from the temporary to the eternal, from the world of action to the world of consequence, and from the life of choice to the life of result.
