The Battle of the Trench: Al-Ahzab

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

In the fifth year after the Hijrah, according to the transmitted accounts, the young community of Madinah faced the greatest military threat of its existence. A coalition of tribes — the Quraysh of Makkah, the Ghatafan, and their allies — marched on the city with an army the tradition numbers in the thousands, while Madinah could field only a few hundred defenders. The siege that followed was the crisis the Quran preserves in Surah Al-Ahzab, the Surah of the Confederates, in verses that have never lost their power: "They came upon you from above you and from below you, and when the eyes rolled in terror and the hearts rose to the throats, and you thought thoughts about Allah" (33:10).
