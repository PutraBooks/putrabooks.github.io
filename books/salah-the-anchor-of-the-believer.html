Salah: The Anchor of the Believer

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Salah: The Anchor of the Believer walks the prayer completely. Before: the command to establish (4:103, 20:14), wudu from 5:6, the times, the adhan, and readiness. During: every step from takbir to salam with its anchor and meaning; Al-Fatihah as a verse-by-verse conversation; the common surahs — Ikhlas, Falaq, Nas, Kawthar, Asr — with what they mean; and the duas, fixed and open, from the tradition and the Quran. After: the closing dhikr, the five as the day's anchors, the congregation and Friday. Then the two chapters every reader needs: why people are lazy to pray — eight Quranic diagnoses, from the heavy-for-the-unhumbled (2:45) to the depressed life of the turned-away (20:124) to the whisperer's deferrals — and the motivation plan: ten moves from the Quran that turn the couch into a prayer mat. Closing with the lifetime of prayer: youth, travel, fear, sickness, age, until the certainty (15:99). New in this edition: the full map of prayer types (Chapter 5) and the traveler's prayer — qasar, jama', and conveyance rulings (Chapter 10).
