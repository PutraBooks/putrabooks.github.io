Trust: The Surrender of the Soul

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran describes trust — *Tawakkul* — as the reliance of the servant on the Lord, the surrender of the soul to the Master, and the dependence of the heart on the Source. The Quran says: "And whoever trusts in Allah, He is sufficient for him" (65:3). Trust is the surrender — the surrender that lets go of the worry, the surrender that lets go of the anxiety, and the surrender that lets go of the control. The trust is not the passivity but the reliance — the reliance that acts and surrenders, the reliance that works and trusts, and the reliance that strives and depends. The person who trusts is the person who does their part and leaves the result to Allah.
