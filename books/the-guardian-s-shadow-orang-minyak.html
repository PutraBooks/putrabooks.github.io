The Guardian's Shadow: Orang Minyak

In the darkness of the kampung, something slick moves — a man glistening with oil, supernaturally fast, invisible when chased. The orang minyak — the Oily Man — is Malay folklore's strangest predator: bound to a ritual diet and black magic, hunting at night, breaking the peace of homes across generations. This book traces the oily man from village legend to 1950s film icon to modern campus panic, and examines what the slick intruder reveals about fear of violation.
