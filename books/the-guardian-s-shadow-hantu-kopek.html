The Guardian's Shadow: Hantu Kopek

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

An old woman waits at the edge of the village after dark, searching for children who stay out too late. The hantu kopek, the pendulous-breasted ghost of Malay tradition, is a bogeywoman: she carries children away beneath her, and every kampung grandmother used her name to bring children home by sundown. This book examines the legend, its parallels across the Malay world, and the many working roles of the female night-spirit in keeping children safe.
