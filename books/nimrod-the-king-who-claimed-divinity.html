Nimrod: The King Who Claimed Divinity

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

A king who claimed the power over life and death argued with Ibrahim about his Lord — and the fire built to destroy the prophet was commanded to be coolness and peace. It explores the Quranic account of the king who argued with Ibrahim (2:258), the fire that was built to destroy the prophet (21:51-70, 37:83-98), the sign of the four birds (2:260), the comparison with Pharaoh, and the fall of the tyrant — all told as a close reading of the Quranic text with attention to the Arabic and the moral lessons it contains.
