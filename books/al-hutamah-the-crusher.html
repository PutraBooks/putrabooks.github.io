Al-Hutamah: The Crusher

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran describes Al-Hutamah — the Crusher — as one of the names of the Fire, emphasizing the shattering, breaking, and crushing nature of the punishment. The word appears in the Quran: "No! Indeed, it will be the Crusher, a Fire that strips away" (104:4-5). The name *Al-Hutamah* carries the meaning of a fire that crushes — a fire that breaks what is hard, shatters what is strong, and crushes what is proud. The image is the shattering: the person who was proud in this world is crushed in the next, and the hardness that was the arrogance is broken by the fire that is the Crusher.
