The Presidential Address

A presidential address can move markets with words whose implementation is never guaranteed — drugs priced on a sentence, sectors repriced on a threat, and months later, some of it never happens. The Presidential Address is the risk framework for trading policy speeches: the podium print's anatomy (the scheduled ritual versus the surprise edition), the policy-domain channel map — healthcare, trade, defense, tech — the words-implementation gap with its documented base rates, the case files from the pharma-pricing decade to the February 2020 address the market ignored at its cost, and the positioning discipline for ritual events: what to do before the words, in the minutes after, and in the weeks that decide whether the words were real. The market's word-driven events, traded with the skepticism the record earns. The Presidential Address is volume 14 of the Trading Impact News series: one market-moving release at a time, what it is, when it lands, and how traders position around it. DRM-free EPUB and PDF.
