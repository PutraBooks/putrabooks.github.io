The Guardian's Shadow: Hantu Air

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Don't swim at dusk. Don't go where the river bends. The water takes what it wants. Hantu air — the water ghost — lives in rivers, mines pools, and sea shallows across the Malay world, pulling swimmers under to replace the drowned. Every kampung knew its dangerous bend. This book surveys the water-spirits of Malay tradition: their many forms and names, the taboos protecting swimmers and fishermen, and the psychology of danger the legends encode.
