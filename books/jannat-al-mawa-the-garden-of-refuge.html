The Quran describes Jannat al-Ma'wa — the Garden of Refuge — as the final home, the place of return, the destination of the journey that every soul is on. The word *Ma'wa* appears in the Quran as the name of a garden whose defining characteristic is its quality as a refuge — the home that the believer returns to after the journey of life, the shelter that receives the person who has been seeking safety, the dwelling that offers the rest that no other dwelling can provide.
