The Guardian's Shadow: Orang Bunian

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

In the deep jungle, Malay folklore says, live people you cannot see — the orang bunian, a hidden nation with houses, markets, and royalty, alongside ours and invisible to it. They invite humans to feast, keep some for years, and return them with gifts and gaps in time. This book examines Malaysia's hidden people: the encounters that built the legend, the abductions and marriages across the veil, and what the bunian tradition says about the forest's power over the mind.
