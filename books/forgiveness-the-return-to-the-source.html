Forgiveness: The Return to the Source

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran describes forgiveness — *Tawbah* — as the return of the servant to the Lord after the fall, the turning back of the sinner to the Source after the straying, and the coming home of the lost to the Master after the wandering. The Quran says: "Say: O My servants who have transgressed against themselves, do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins. Indeed, it is He who is the Forgiving, the Merciful" (39:53). Forgiveness is the return — the return that is the turning, the return that is the coming back, and the return that is the homecoming. The forgiveness is not only the pardon but the relationship — the relationship that is restored, the relationship that is renewed, and the relationship that is revived.
