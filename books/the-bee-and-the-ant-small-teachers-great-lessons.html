The Bee and the Ant: Small Teachers, Great Lessons

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran mentions many animals, but two of the smallest receive the most detailed treatment: the bee and the ant. The bee is the only animal the Quran says received *wahy* — divine inspiration. The ant is the only animal the Quran records speaking in articulate, reasoned speech to its community. Between them, these two tiny creatures carry some of the Quran's most profound lessons about divine communication, social organization, intelligence, and the relationship between size and significance.
