The Vain Duelist: The Personality in Love With Its Own Excellence

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Somewhere between the wardrobe and the flourish, the duel became a photo shoot. The vain duelist is a portrait of excellence cultivated as a portrait: the child whose beauty of motion drew crowds, and who learned to live in the sightlines. The book is fair to the craft — the excellence is real, and the flourish is earned, and there is a genuine artistry in a self curated this well. It is equally fair about the mirror's economy: the victory incomplete without the witness, the rival recruited mainly as a lens, the love that must be aimed, adoringly, back at the source. The middle chapters follow the type through teams, work, and romance, where the sightline quietly governs everything. The later chapters turn the frame: the excellence kept, the mirror set down, and the discovery that a self admired for its interior needs no audience at all — which is, for this constitution, the last duel worth winning. An original personality study in the Archetype Series; it references no real persons, products, or properties.
