The Fed Chair Press Conference

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

There is a half hour, eight times a year, in which the person who controls the world's reserve currency answers live questions without a script. Markets hang on adjectives — and this is the volume for the Fed Chair press conference: how the podium's sentence structure signals conviction (the pause grammar, the repetition tell), how a statement's meaning gets rewritten thirty minutes after release by a single answer, the second-wave trade the first five minutes hide, and the case files where a press conference turned the decision into a different one — the 2019 'mid-cycle adjustment' that wasn't, the 2021 'transitory' retirement. The conference is where the statement gets its meaning — this teaches you to watch it the way the desks do: in words, in tone, and in the pauses between. The Fed Chair Press Conference is volume 3 of the Trading Impact News series: one market-moving release at a time, what it is, when it lands, and how traders position around it.
