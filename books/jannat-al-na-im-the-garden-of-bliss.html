Jannat al-Na'im: The Garden of Bliss

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran describes Jannat al-Na'im — the Garden of Bliss — as the destination of those who lived in faith and died in righteousness. The word *Na'im* appears in the Quran as the name of a garden whose defining characteristic is the continuous, unending experience of joy. The Quran says: "Indeed, the righteous will be in pleasure, and indeed, the wicked will be in Hellfire" (82:13-14). The word used for pleasure here is *na'im* — the bliss that is the reward of the righteous, the joy that is the state of the Garden, the happiness that does not fade.
