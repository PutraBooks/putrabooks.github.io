The Guardian's Shadow: Badi

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

You mocked a crocodile, ignored a warning, or walked where you shouldn't — and now the sickness has no ordinary cause. Badi is the Malay concept of spiritual backlash: harm that flows from disrespect, contact, and contagion, carried by animals, places, and the offended dead. This book examines the unseen current of Malay folk belief: how badi is caught, recognized, and cured, the creatures that carry it, and the etiquette of caution that kept villages safe from it.
