The Cursed Hunter: The Personality That Becomes the Thing It Hunts

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The enemy's methods worked — and were learned, and practiced, and mastered. The cursed hunter is the study of contamination by the quarry: the pursuit that consumes the pursuer, each victory over the darkness bought with another piece of its currency, until the line between hunter and hunted requires a mirror to find. The book follows the apprenticeship with respect, because the type is genuinely good at this: nobody catches the predator like someone who understands it completely. It follows the cost with the same precision: the paranoia that keeps hunting after the hunt ends, the tactics used at home because they worked in the wild, the self quietly becoming the final quarry. The later chapters break the curse without breaking the hunter: the skill kept, the darkness set down, and the discovery that the person who knows the monster best is the one most qualified to refuse the job of being it. An original personality study in the Archetype Series; it references no real persons, products, or properties.
