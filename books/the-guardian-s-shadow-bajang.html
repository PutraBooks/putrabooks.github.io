The Guardian's Shadow: Bajang

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

In the branches of the kemayan tree, a child spirit waits — a bajang, in the shape of a wild boy, sent out at night to sicken babies and terrify households. Malaysian villages protected newborns with charms and knives beneath pillows, for centuries of nights. This book traces the bajang from its origins in stillbirth and abduction legend to the familiar kept in a bamboo vessel, and asks what the child-demon reveals about how communities feared for their children.
