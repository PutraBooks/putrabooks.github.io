The Steadfast Guardian

S$1.90 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Everyone leans on this person; almost nobody leans toward them. Here is the psychology of the steady one at the perimeter — the one who remembered your emergency, covered your shift, and held the line so quietly that the line became invisible. This book takes the reliability apart like a craft: the vigilance that never clocks out, the load quietly over-carried, the pride in being the wall the family leans on, and the loneliness that grows on the safe side of it. It follows the type to work, where they are the team's floor, and into love, where they are adored and rarely asked. The later chapters do the harder work: why trusting feels like gambling to this constitution, and what it takes to let the shield down on purpose. An original personality study in the Archetype Series, written for the person who holds everyone up — and for the people who should start holding them. It references no real persons, products, or properties.
