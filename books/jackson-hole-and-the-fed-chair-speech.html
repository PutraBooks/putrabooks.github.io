Jackson Hole and the Fed Chair Speech

S$1.90 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Every August, while much of the financial world is on holiday, a mountain valley in Wyoming becomes the single most-watched location in global finance. The Fed Chair's Jackson Hole speech has announced policy regime changes with every desk listening — and this volume teaches you to listen the way they do: the speech's anatomy and what its themes have historically signaled, the market channels that move before and after, the case files of past speeches, from the 2020 framework revision to the 2022 hawkish shock that crushed a rally, and the preparation framework that turns a vague mountain retreat into a planned event instead of an improvised one. The market's quietest calendar week, its biggest potential signal — how the message is telegraphed, how the new framework is separated from the ritual, and how a speech that mentions no numbers can move all of them. Jackson Hole and the Fed Chair Speech is volume 1 of the Trading Impact News series: one market-moving release at a time, what it is, when it lands, and how traders position around it.
