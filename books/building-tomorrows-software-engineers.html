Building Tomorrow's Software Engineers

S$1.90 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

A piece of software is a collection of promises, and most of the trade's trouble begins when a promise is kept in somebody's head instead of in the work. This book describes how that work is actually done: the daily practice of the people who author those promises and keep them, the territory the work covers, the rewards the career actually pays, and the differences the machine era is already making to a trade whose ordinary code can now be drafted for it. The chapters proceed as the collection proceeds: first the contract itself, then the folklores that misdescribe the trade, the routes by which people enter it, the shape of its ordinary day, the craft at the bench, the strain, the conversations that surround the code, the instruments that keep it honest, one practitioner's composite year, and the arc of a career. Written for people considering, entering, or managing software engineering work. Part of Building Tomorrow's People. Want to read before you buy?
