Building the Metaverse of Tomorrow

The metaverse is a persistent, shared, embodied world - a computed place that does not pause when you leave it, that strangers occupy at the same time you do, that you enter as a body rather than a username, and whose value compounds precisely because it is all three at once. This structural guide treats the metaverse as the construction project the decade keeps promising and keeps defunding: the four-clause contract and its impostors, the five stacks every working world must carry - presence, world, identity, economy, governance - the body's arbitration of presence, the world's constitutional memory of state, the portable self across worlds, the treaty layer of interoperability and its honest ladder, the legislated scarcity of the made economy, the governed crowd and the appeal that worked, the enterprise envelope deployed where the structure fits, and the five-line operating program that lets a place survive its founder. A structural blueprint for building - and governing - the metaverse of tomorrow.
