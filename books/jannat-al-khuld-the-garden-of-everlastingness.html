Jannat al-Khuld: The Garden of Everlastingness

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran describes Jannat al-Khuld — the Garden of Everlastingness — as the destination whose defining characteristic is the absence of ending. The word *Khuld* appears in the Quran as the name of a garden whose essential quality is everlastingness — the life that does not terminate, the joy that does not cease, and the existence that does not come to a close. The Quran says: "And no soul knows what has been hidden for them of comfort for eyes, as a reward for what they used to do" (32:17). The hidden comfort — the reward that exceeds every expectation — is the *Khuld*: the everlastingness that is the life of the Garden.
