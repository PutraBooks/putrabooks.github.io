The Prohibition of Extravagance: The Waste of the Excess

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran's prohibition of extravagance is part of its broader vision of the middle way — the path between the two extremes of excess and deprivation. The Quran says: "And the believers are those who, when they spend, are neither excessive nor stingy, but are ever, between that, moderate" (25:67). The verse captures the essence of the Quranic ethic of wealth: the believer is the person who spends moderately — not lavishly and not miserly, not in excess and not in deprivation, but in the balance that Allah has established. The middle way is not merely a financial principle; it is a spiritual principle — the principle that every capacity, every resource, and every blessing is to be used in accordance with the measure that Allah has set.
