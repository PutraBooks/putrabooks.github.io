The Quran describes As-Sa'ir — the Blazing Fire — as one of the names of the Fire, emphasizing the kindled, ignited, and ascending quality of the punishment. The word appears in the Quran: "Indeed, your Lord is Allah, who created the heavens and the earth in six days and then established Himself above the Throne. He covers the day with the night, which hastens to follow it. And He created the sun, the moon, and the stars, subjected by His command. Unquestionably, His is the creation and the command. Blessed is Allah, Lord of the worlds" (7:54, broader context) — and the mention of As-Sa'ir appears in various verses throughout the Quran as a name of the punishment that awaits those who reject.
