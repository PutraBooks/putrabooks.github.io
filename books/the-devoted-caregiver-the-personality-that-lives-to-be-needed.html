The Devoted Caregiver: The Personality That Lives to Be Needed

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

This is the person who notices you are tired before you do — and who has quietly organized their whole life around that noticing. The devoted caregiver gets a full portrait here: the attunement that amounts to genius, the service that amounts to competence, the household that runs on their predictions. The book honors the gift before it does anything else. Then it does the other thing: it shows how the same web that holds everyone up can hold the weaver hostage — the needs that must keep existing for the identity to stand, the resentment nobody is allowed to see, the self that has become a function of other people's weather. The growth chapters walk from hostage to choice: the discovery that care given freely and care given to be needed are different crafts, and that the caregiver who also receives is not lesser but complete. An original personality study in the Archetype Series; it references no real persons, products, or properties.
