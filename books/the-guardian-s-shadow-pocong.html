The Guardian's Shadow: Pocong

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

In the shroud it was buried in — white cloth, knotted at head and feet — the dead man returns, hopping through the village because the knots were never untied. The pocong is modern Indonesia's most visible ghost: a wrapped corpse leaping in the dark, asking to be released. This book examines the pocong's roots in burial practice and Islamic obligation, the explosion of the legend through film and television, and why a body wrapped wrong disturbs the living so much.
