Animals of the Quran: Creatures as Divine Signs

The Quran mentions animals in dozens of surahs — the camel, the cow, the fish, the bird, the dog, the horse, the bee, the ant, the spider, the elephant, the monkey, the pig, the locust, the snake, and the sheep. Some are named as individuals with stories. Others are named as categories with functions. Some are honored. Some are punished. Some are teachers. Some are miracles.
