Remembrance: The Breath of the Heart

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Quran describes remembrance — *Dhikr* — as the awareness of Allah in the heart, the mention of Allah on the tongue, and the consciousness of Allah in the life. The Quran says: "Therefore remember Me, I will remember you. And be grateful to Me and do not deny Me" (2:152). Remembrance is the awareness — the awareness that keeps the heart connected to the Lord, the awareness that keeps the tongue mentioning the Master, and the awareness that keeps the life oriented to the Source. The remembrance is not only the ritual but the living — the living that is the constant awareness, the living that is the ongoing mention, and the living that is the perpetual consciousness.
