The Quiet Specialist: The Personality That Wins the Long Game

You will not notice this person for years, and then you will never un-notice them again. This is a study of the quiet specialist — the colleague who said little, shipped steadily, and turned out to have been compounding the whole time. Chapters on the engine, the craft, the cost, the matchups, and the growth arc that ends in earned presence. An original personality study in the Archetype Series; it references no real persons, products, or properties.
