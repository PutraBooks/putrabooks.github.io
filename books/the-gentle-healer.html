The Gentle Healer

S$1.90 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The house has one person who arrives exactly when the room is breaking. This book is about them — the gentle healer, whose mercy became a vocation before they were old enough to spell it. The portrait is tender and unsparing at once: the triage calm, the touch that steadies, the presence that mends, and beneath it the founding wound that taught the child that healing others was the safest room in the house. The book prices the exemption honestly — the healer whose own back goes unexamined, the bed kept empty by policy, the grief worked through other people's recoveries. The later chapters write the correction this type never thinks to need: mercy that includes the self, the medicine turned home, and the startling discovery that the healer who is also healed does not lose the hands. An original personality study in the Archetype Series; it references no real persons, products, or properties.
