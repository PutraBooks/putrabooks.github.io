The Guardian's Shadow: Polong

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Kept in a bottle, fed daily from the keeper's own finger, the polong is a tiny vampiric spirit sent out to punish a village's enemies. Its victims fall with unexplainable fits and blood loss — and folklore insisted the polong would name its sender if questioned correctly. This book traces the polong through Malay village justice: how the spirit was made, kept, dispatched and confessed, and what a bottled demon says about revenge in communities without courts.
