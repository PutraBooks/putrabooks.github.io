The Mother of Musa: The Trusting Mother

$0.99 · DRM-free EPUB + PDF

The Mother of Musa: The Trusting Mother explores the story of the mother of Musa as the Quran presents it across Surah Al-Qasas (28:7-13) and Surah Taha (20:38-40): the divine inspiration, the casting of the child into the river, the finding by Pharaoh's family, the return of the child to his mother, the pairing with Asiya as the two mothers of Musa, and the comparison with Hajar and Maryam as models of maternal faith. The book covers the psychology of trust, the relationship between human effort and divine promise, and the theme that the mother's faith is the foundation of the prophet's mission.
