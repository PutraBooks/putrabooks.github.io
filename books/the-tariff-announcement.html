The Tariff Announcement

DRM-free EPUB + PDF

Since 2025, the tariff announcement has been a macro force: a single podium statement repricing currencies, equities, and supply chains across the world. The Tariff Announcement trades the structure: the threat-delivered pattern — announcement, pause, escalation, graduation — the leak ladder that moves prices before the podium, the market channel map (importers, exporters, the dollar's haven paradox, the retaliation cycle), the case files from the 2018 steel round to the April 2025 graduation, and the machinery grading that separates words from consequences: which tariffs bind, which are theater, and how long each takes to reach prices. The event class that rewired global trade — traded with its actual mechanics instead of its headlines. The Tariff Announcement is volume 15 of the Trading Impact News series: one market-moving release at a time, what it is, when it lands, and how traders position around it. DRM-free EPUB and PDF.
